Biography

A career in public service

Dave Levac was first elected as MPP for Brant in 1999 and has been re-elected four times since. In the Legislature he has served as Public Safety & Security Critic, Chief Government Whip, and chair of several committees, and he was Parliamentary Assistant to two ministers. He was also Vice-Chair of the Ontario-US and Ontario-Quebec parliamentary associations. He became Ontario’s 41st Speaker in November 2011 and was re-elected to the role in July 2014, a position that includes chairing the Board of Internal Economy and acting as the province’s parliamentary diplomat.

Before entering politics, Levac spent 12 years teaching elementary and secondary school and became an elementary school principal in 1989. He developed conflict resolution programs in that role and received a Distinguished Teacher Award in 1994. He also took an active leadership role in his community through local non-profit organizations.
